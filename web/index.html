Books and reading history

Import a bundle below to edit its books. Pages are inclusive; audiobook fields accept seconds, HH:MM or HH:MM:SS. Save changes before switching apps. Keep only one active editor, including browser tabs.

      Reading schedule

      Set the plan dates and rest days, then calculate the baseline schedule. Rest days reduce the days available for reading, but they do not move the baseline dates. Books in a simultaneous group share a start and finish while their daily targets are split by remaining work. A deadline override gives one book an independent schedule until you clear it. The baseline schedule stays saved until you calculate it again.

          Reading projections

          Each projection estimates when a book will be finished from its recorded reading history, separately from its baseline schedule and deadline. The estimated finish range shows how uncertain that estimate is; it is not a guarantee. Projections never change the baseline schedule or the required pace.

          Charts

          All is off by default: charts show books with progress that are not finished. Plan and actual use the left axis; read/required per day uses the right axis. Projection ranges are estimates, not guarantees.

          Next quarter

          Plan the books for the next quarter here. When the quarter starts, unfinished current books carry forward first with their history and stable IDs, completed books leave the plan, and the baseline schedule is recalculated. The plan activates automatically when you open or use the app on or after its start date.

            Summary settings

            Transfer desktop bundle

            Select all four JSON files to validate and replace the stored bundle. This does not change the text draft, and importing a bundle replaces the reading schedule saved for the previous bundle. Export a ZIP to transfer the bundle back to desktop.

            CSV compatibility

            CSV replaces the current plan's books and progress, but contains no reading history, stable IDs, overrides or next-quarter plan. Keep a bundle ZIP backup first. Existing next-quarter books are retained. Use CSV only with trusted reading apps; spreadsheet software can interpret titles as formulas.
